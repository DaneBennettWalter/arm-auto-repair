## Option 1: HUD-Code Manufactured Home (RECOMMENDED)

**What it is:** Factory-built home constructed after June 15, 1976, meeting HUD federal standards

**Why it works:**
- Texas state law (SB 785, effective Sept 1, 2026) mandates cities allow these in at least one residential zone
- Robstown cannot ban them outright
- Treated similarly to traditional construction once properly installed

**Requirements:**
1. **Zoning verification** - Confirm 706 E Ave G zoning allows manufactured homes
   - Contact: Robstown Building & Development, 361-387-4589 ext. 135
   - Email: qc_hub@cityofrobstown.com
   
2. **Lot minimums** (standard Robstown residential):
   - Width: 50 feet minimum
   - Area: 6,000 sq ft minimum
   - Setbacks: 25 ft front yard (varies by R-1/R-2/R-3 district)

3. **Installation standards:**
   - HUD federal installation requirements
   - Permanent foundation (TDI-approved)
   - Anchoring/tie-down for local wind zone
   - Utility connections per state/local codes
   - Licensed installer (TDHCA-approved)

4. **Permits required:**
   - Building permit
   - Electrical permit
   - Plumbing permit
   - Proof of TDI compliance (W.P.I-1 form or TDI engineer letter)

5. **Application process:**
   - City has 45 days to deny in writing with reasons
   - If no denial in 45 days = automatically approved
   - Cannot require special use permit if not required for other residential in same zone

**Cost considerations:**
- Lower than traditional construction
- Foundation work is significant expense
- Professional installation required (licensed)

## Option 2: Mobile Home Park (REQUIRES REZONING)

**What it is:** Designated trailer camp/mobile home court in B-2 (Secondary & Highway Business) zone

**Why it's harder:**
- 706 E Ave G is likely R-1/R-2/R-3 residential
- Requires rezoning to B-2 = two public hearings (Planning & Zoning + City Council)
- Community opposition likely

**If you pursue this route:**

**Regulations (Robstown Ordinance § 14A-6.3):**
- **Density:** Maximum 10 mobile homes per acre
- **Setbacks:**
  - Front yard: 25 feet minimum
  - Rear yard: 10 feet minimum
  - Side yard: 6 feet minimum
  - Total side yards: 20 feet unobstructed
- **Access:** Must be from arterial highway or secondary thoroughfare
  - Interior drives: 24 feet wide minimum
  - Individual spaces cannot have direct street access outside park
- **Allowed homes:** Both pre-1976 mobile homes and HUD-code manufactured homes

**Process:**
1. Apply for zoning change with Planning & Zoning Commission
2. Public hearing before Planning & Zoning
3. Public hearing before City Council
4. Site plan approval
5. Building permits for infrastructure

## What's NOT Allowed

### RVs / Travel Trailers as Dwellings
**Confirmed Prohibitions (Article 12.05):**
- **Cannot park on public streets/alleys for more than 48 hours** (general vehicle limit)
- **Trailers and campers prohibited on public street rights-of-way, public streets, and alleyways** (mounted or unmounted)
- Cannot be occupied as a dwelling

**Reported Regulations (citation unverified):**
- May be allowed on private residential property on improved surface for temporary parking (72 hours referenced in secondary sources for loading, cleaning, trip prep, winterizing)
- Specific ordinance section for residential property RV storage not confirmed

**Action Required:**
Contact Robstown Inspections Department (361-387-4589 ext. 135) to confirm:
- Whether RVs can be temporarily parked on residential lots
- Duration limits for temporary parking
- Surface requirements (improved vs unimproved)
- Any length restrictions
- Enforcement policy on RV use as dwellings

### Pre-1976 Mobile Homes on Individual Lots
- Robstown ordinance explicitly prohibits "trailers" (pre-1976 mobile homes)
- Only allowed in designated mobile home parks
- Cannot be placed on individual residential lots, even temporarily
