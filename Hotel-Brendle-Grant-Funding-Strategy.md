## Part 1: Historic Preservation Tax Credits (45% Total)

### Federal Historic Preservation Tax Credit (20%)

**Program:** Federal Rehabilitation Tax Credit  
**Administrator:** National Park Service (NPS) + IRS, coordinated through Texas Historical Commission (THC)  
**Benefit:** 20% federal income tax credit  
**Established:** 1976

#### Eligibility Requirements:
1. **Building must be:**
   - Listed in National Register of Historic Places, OR
   - Determined eligible for National Register listing
   
2. **Property must be:**
   - Income-producing (Hotel Brendle qualifies ✅)
   - Used for commercial, industrial, agricultural, or rental residential purposes
   
3. **Rehabilitation must:**
   - Meet Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation
   - Be substantial (generally equal to or exceed building's adjusted basis)
   
4. **Documentation:**
   - Part 1: Evaluation of Significance (building qualifies as historic)
   - Part 2: Description of Rehabilitation (proposed work meets standards)
   - Part 3: Request for Certification of Completed Work

#### Application Process:
1. Submit applications electronically to Texas Historical Commission
2. THC reviews and forwards recommendation to NPS
3. NPS makes final certification decision
4. Submit IRS Form 3468 with tax return after project completion

**Source:** https://thc.texas.gov/preserve/grants-tax-credits-and-funding/historic-preservation-tax-credits/federal-rehabilitation  
**NPS Details:** https://www.nps.gov/subjects/taxincentives/index.htm

---

### Texas Historic Preservation Tax Credit (25%)

**Program:** Texas Historic Preservation Tax Credit Program  
**Administrator:** Texas Historical Commission (THC) + Texas Comptroller  
**Benefit:** 25% state tax credit (transferable/sellable)  
**Established:** 2015 (Texas Legislature HB 500, 83rd Session)

#### Eligibility Requirements:
1. **Building must be:**
   - Listed in National Register of Historic Places, OR
   - Determined eligible for National Register, OR
   - Designated as Recorded Texas Historic Landmark (RTHL), OR
   - State Antiquities Landmark (SAL)
   
2. **Property can be:**
   - Income-producing (Hotel Brendle qualifies ✅)
   - Non-profit use
   
3. **Rehabilitation must:**
   - Meet Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation
   - Minimum Qualified Rehabilitation Expenditures (QREs) required
   
4. **Credit Application:**
   - Applied against Texas Business Franchise Tax OR Texas Insurance Premium Tax
   - **Credit is freely transferable** = can be sold for cash value
   - Most applicants sell the credit certificate for market value

#### Key Advantage:
Because Texas has no state income tax, the credit can be **sold to other businesses** who can use it against their franchise or insurance tax liability. This provides **immediate cash benefit** rather than waiting for tax liability.

**Source:** https://thc.texas.gov/preserve/grants-tax-credits-and-funding/historic-preservation-tax-credits/texas-historic  
**Legal Code:** Texas Administrative Code, Title 13, Part II, Chapter 13  
**Legislative Authority:** HB 500 (83rd Session) - http://www.capitol.state.tx.us/tlodocs/83R/billtext/html/HB00500F.htm

## Part 2: National Register of Historic Places Listing

**Program:** National Register of Historic Places  
**Administrator:** National Park Service (NPS), coordinated by Texas Historical Commission (THC)  
**Benefit:** Federal recognition, gateway to tax credits and grants  
**Cost:** Application fees vary (typically $1,000-$5,000 for professional preparation)

### Eligibility Criteria:

Properties must be at least **50 years old** (with rare exceptions) and meet one or more of these criteria:

**A.** Associated with events that have made a significant contribution to broad patterns of history

**B.** Associated with lives of persons significant in our past

**C.** Embody distinctive characteristics of a type, period, or method of construction; represent work of a master; possess high artistic values; or represent a significant and distinguishable entity

**D.** Have yielded, or may be likely to yield, information important in prehistory or history

**Hotel Brendle Context:**
- Built: [NEED YEAR - likely 1920s-1940s based on "historic" status]
- Age: Likely 75-100+ years old ✅
- Significance: Historic hotel in Robstown (birthplace of Texas Hold'em), community anchor
- Architectural: [NEED DETAILS - style, notable features, original design elements]

### Application Process (Sequential Steps):

**Step 1: Pre-Application Research**
   - Historical research on building
   - Architectural documentation
   - Photograph documentation (interior/exterior)
   - Property boundary determination
   
**Step 2: Application Preparation**
   - Complete National Register nomination form
   - Write historical narrative
   - Document architectural significance
   - Prepare photographs and maps
   - **Recommendation:** Hire professional consultant familiar with THC requirements
   
**Step 3: THC Staff Review**
   - Staff historian reviews nomination
   - May request additional information
   - Recommendation to State Board of Review
   
**Step 4: State Board of Review**
   - Presentation to board (quarterly meetings)
   - Board votes on recommendation
   - If approved, forwarded to NPS
   
**Step 5: National Park Service Review**
   - NPS Keeper of National Register makes final decision
   - Listing is official upon Keeper signature

**Source:** https://thc.texas.gov/preserve/designate-historic-properties/national-register-historic-places

## Part 10: Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Risk 1: National Register Rejection
**Probability:** Low if properly researched and prepared  
**Impact:** High (blocks both tax credits)  
**Mitigation:**
- Hire experienced consultant
- Strong historical documentation
- Architectural significance documentation
- Pre-application consultation with THC staff

### Risk 2: Tax Credit Part 2 Rejection (Plans Don't Meet Standards)
**Probability:** Medium if architect unfamiliar with standards  
**Impact:** High (delays construction, requires redesign)  
**Mitigation:**
- Hire architect with historic preservation experience
- Early consultation with THC during design
- Conservative approach to character-defining features

### Risk 3: Construction Cost Overruns Reduce Effective Benefit
**Probability:** Medium (construction always has unknowns)  
**Impact:** Medium (benefit % stays same but cash flow impacted)  
**Mitigation:**
- Detailed cost estimating upfront
- Contingency in budget
- Phase renovation if needed

### Risk 4: Texas Tax Credit Market Value Below Expected
**Probability:** Low (mature market, stable pricing)  
**Impact:** Medium (receive less cash than expected)  
**Mitigation:**
- Engage credit broker early
- Understand current market pricing
- Build conservative pricing (85-90%) into pro forma

### Risk 5: Government Review Process Delays
**Probability:** Medium (government review processes can be unpredictable)  
**Impact:** Medium (cash flow timing)  
**Mitigation:**
- Maintain strong documentation to avoid delays
- Be immediately responsive to agency requests
- Work with experienced consultants who know the process
