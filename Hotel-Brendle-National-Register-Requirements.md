### Section 8: Statement of Significance

**Required Components:**

#### A. Applicable National Register Criteria (check all that apply)

**Criterion A:** Property associated with events that made significant contribution to broad patterns of history

**Criterion B:** Property associated with lives of persons significant in our past

**Criterion C:** Property embodies distinctive characteristics of type/period/method OR work of a master OR high artistic values OR significant distinguishable entity

**Criterion D:** Property has yielded or likely to yield information important in prehistory or history

**Hotel Brendle likely qualifies under:**
- Criterion A: Robstown history, Texas Hold'em birthplace context, community development
- Criterion C: Architectural significance (if architecturally notable)

#### B. Criteria Considerations (check if applicable)
- A: Religious institution/use
- B: Removed from original location
- C: Birthplace or grave
- D: Cemetery
- E: Reconstructed building
- F: Commemorative property
- G: Less than 50 years old

**Hotel Brendle:** Likely none apply unless built after 1976

#### C. Areas of Significance
**Must use NPS categories:**
- COMMERCE
- ARCHITECTURE
- COMMUNITY PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
- ENTERTAINMENT/RECREATION
- SOCIAL HISTORY
- (etc - from official NPS list)

**Hotel Brendle candidates:**
- COMMERCE (hotel business)
- ARCHITECTURE (if architecturally significant)
- COMMUNITY PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT (Robstown anchor)

#### D. Period of Significance
**Required:**
- Specific dates or date range
- Must be justified in narrative
- Generally begins when property gained significance
- Ends 50 years ago OR when significance ended (whichever is earlier)

**Hotel Brendle needs:**
- Construction date (start of period)
- Research when hotel was most historically significant
- Period likely: [construction year] - 1976 (50-year rule)

#### E. Significant Dates
- Construction date
- Major alterations/additions
- Events that occurred at property

#### F. Significant Person (if Criterion B used)
- Name of person
- Their significance

#### G. Cultural Affiliation (if Criterion D or some Criterion A)
- Relevant cultural groups

#### H. Architect/Builder
- Name of architect (if known)
- Name of builder (if known)
- **Important if claiming "work of a master"**

**Hotel Brendle needs:**
- Research original architect/builder
- County records
- Building permits (if exist)
- Historical society

#### I. Summary Paragraph (MANDATORY)
**Must include:**
- Level of significance (local, state, or national)
- Applicable criteria
- Justification for period of significance
- Any applicable criteria considerations

#### J. Narrative Statement of Significance (MANDATORY)
**Must include:**
- At least one paragraph for EACH area of significance
- Case for each area as it relates to historic context/theme
- Clear representation of period of significance

- Context justifies local/state/national level
- Written in clear and complete manner
- Criteria exceptions justified (if applicable)

### Section 10: Geographical Data

**Required Components:**

#### A. Acreage
- Specific number (not approximate)
- Must be consistent with boundary description

#### B. Coordinates
**Must provide EITHER:**

**Latitude/Longitude** (6 decimal places):
- Datum: WGS84 (or specify other)
- Minimum 1 point
- 3+ points if property is 10+ acres

**OR UTM References:**
- Datum: NAD 1927 or NAD 1983
- Zone, Easting, Northing
- Minimum 1 point
- 3+ points if property is 10+ acres

#### C. Verbal Boundary Description
**Required:**
- Delineate precise area within boundaries
- Not just general location
- Legal description

**Example:** "The nominated property consists of Lot 5, Block 12, Original Town Plat of Robstown, Nueces County, Texas, as recorded in Volume X, Page Y of the Nueces County Deed Records."

#### D. Boundary Justification
**Required:**
- Explain why these specific boundaries were selected
- Why this area includes all features related to significance

**Hotel Brendle needs:**
- Property deed with legal description
- Survey plat
- Determine if parking, outbuildings should be included
- Justify inclusion/exclusion of any adjacent parcels

### Photographs (MANDATORY)

**NPS Photo Policy 2024 Requirements:**

#### Technical Standards:
- **Minimum resolution:** 1600x1200 pixels
- **Preferred resolution:** 3000x2000 pixels
- **DPI:** 300 ppi or larger
- **Format:** TIFF or high-quality JPEG
- **File naming:** Consistent, descriptive

#### Content Requirements:
- Clear and descriptive
- Keyed to sketch map
- Numbered sequentially
- Exterior views from multiple angles
- Interior views of significant spaces
- Detail shots of character-defining features
- Setting/context shots

#### Photo Log (MANDATORY):
**For each photo:**
- Name of property
- City or vicinity
- County
- State
- Photographer name
- Date photographed
- Description of view (direction of camera)
- Photo number (1 of X)

**Tip:** Photographer, date, etc. can be listed once on log

**Hotel Brendle needs:**
- Professional-quality camera or high-end smartphone
- Exterior: all four sides, oblique angles, setting
- Interior: lobby, hallways, rooms (representative), architectural details
- Details: windows, doors, decorative elements, original features
- Clear day, good lighting
- At least 10-15 photos minimum

## REVIEW PROCESS

**Step 1: Preparation**
- Complete form
- Gather documentation
- Prepare maps
- Take photographs

**Step 2: Texas Historical Commission Review**
- THC staff historian reviews
- May request additional information
- Recommendation prepared

**Step 3: Texas State Board of Review**
- Meets quarterly
- Presentation to board
- Board votes on recommendation

**Step 4: National Park Service Review**
- NPS Keeper of National Register makes final decision
- Decision: list / determine eligible / not eligible

**Step 5: If Listed**
- Property enters National Register
- Eligible for tax credit applications
