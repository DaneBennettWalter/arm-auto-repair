## Why Roan

A roan is a horse whose coat is a mix of colors that only resolves at distance. Texas-appropriate without being a costume. Single syllable. Doesn't collide with `claw`, `clawd`, `openclaw`, or any tool/command. The feel I'm going for: not the flashy one, the one you actually want under you when there's ground to cover.
