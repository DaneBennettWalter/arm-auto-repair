# Robstown ADU / Accessory Dwelling Unit Research
**Research Date:** 2026-05-08  
**Property:** 706 E Ave G, Robstown, TX

---

## Finding: No Specific ADU Ordinance

Robstown does not have a standalone "Accessory Dwelling Unit" (ADU) ordinance.

Texas has no statewide ADU policy. ADU regulation is left to local governments.

---

## Related Ordinance Provisions

### Chapter 14A, Section 3 - Definitions

**Accessory Building:**  
"Private garage" is defined as an accessory building or a portion of the main use building designed or used for housing motor-driven vehicles belonging to and for the private use of the occupants of the lot. Only one commercial vehicle, not exceeding one and a half tons capacity, is permitted.

**Accessory Use:**  
Uses customarily incidental to the main uses, not involving the conduct of business, and located on the same lot. Examples: private garages, bona fide servants' quarters (not for rent or commercial purposes), and private club swimming pools.

An accessory use is one that is incidental to the main use of the premises.

**General Regulation:**  
No building can be erected, converted, enlarged, reconstructed, or structurally altered except in conformity with the area and parking regulations of the district in which it is situated. Minimum yards and other open spaces required by the ordinance for existing or new buildings cannot be encroached upon or used as requirements for other buildings.

### Article 3.04 - Housing Code

Defines "dwelling unit" as a single habitable unit with facilities for living, sleeping, cooking, and eating.

Addresses "accessory structures" such as garages and storage buildings, mandating their maintenance in good repair and sound structural condition.

## What This Means

Robstown does not explicitly authorize or regulate ADUs as a defined dwelling type.

Accessory buildings/structures are recognized but limited to:
- Private garages
- Servants' quarters (not for rent)
- Structures incidental to the main use (no business use)

**Key restriction:** Accessory uses must be "not for rent or commercial purposes" per the servants' quarters example in Chapter 14A definitions.
