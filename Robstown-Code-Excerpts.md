# Robstown Municipal Code - Mobile Home & Trailer Regulations
## Exact Text from City Ordinances

---

## Chapter 14A, Section 3 - Definitions

### Definition 35: TRAILER

"Any structure used for living, sleeping, business, or storage purposes, constructed to be towed by a motor vehicle on its own chassis over roads and highways under special permit or license, connected to utilities and designed without a permanent foundation for year-round living as a trailer."

**Exclusions:** The term "trailer" specifically excludes camp cars, house cars, motorcar homes, and pickup campers. It also excludes camping trailers used exclusively for hunting, fishing, or other forms of recreation that are not used for human habitation at any time within the city limits.

**Critical provision:**  
"For the purposes of this ordinance a trailer is not a single-family dwelling and no house trailer shall be located, situated, or placed either temporarily or permanently within the city limits of the City of Robstown, Texas, except in designated trailer camps or trailer parks, and when trailers are sold by a dealer in a business or industrial zone."

## Zoning Districts (Chapter 14A)

The City of Robstown is divided into the following zoning districts:

- **"R-1"** First One-Family Dwelling District
- **"R-2"** Second One-Family Dwelling District
- **"R-3"** Duplex and Apartment District
- **"B-1"** Neighborhood Business District
- **"B-2"** Secondary and Highway Business District
- **"B-3"** Central Business District
- **"I"** Industrial District

All newly annexed territory is initially classified as **"R-1"** First One-Family Dwelling District until it receives permanent zoning from the City of Robstown governing body.

---

## Mobile Home Parks / Trailer Camps

### Permitted Zones
Trailer camps or mobile home courts are permitted in the **"B-2"** Secondary and Highway Business District.

### Regulations for Mobile Home Subdivisions / Parks

**Density:**  
No more than ten (10) mobile homes per acre.

**Yard Requirements:**
- Minimum front yard: 25 feet
- Minimum rear yard: 10 feet
- Minimum side yard: 6 feet
- Total unobstructed side yard requirement: 20 feet

**Access:**  
Access to the mobile home or trailer park must be from an arterial highway or secondary thoroughfare. The number and location of access drives are controlled for traffic safety and protection of surrounding properties. Individual mobile home or trailer spaces shall not have direct access to a street outside the park boundaries. Interior access drives must be at least 24 feet wide.

---

## Residential Lot Dimensions (General Requirements)

Residential lots in the city are generally required to be:
- Width: Not less than 50 feet
- Area: Not less than 6,000 square feet

Corner lots for residential use should have extra width to allow for appropriate building setback and orientation to both streets.

## R-1 First One-Family Dwelling District

### Permitted Uses:
- Single-family dwellings
- Churches
- Public or private schools
- Public parks, playgrounds, golf courses
- Municipal buildings
- Nonprofit libraries or museums
- Police and fire stations
- Farms, nurseries, truck gardens, greenhouses (no sales office)

---

## R-2 Second One-Family Dwelling District

Permits any use allowed in the "R-1" district.

**Height Regulations:**  
Buildings generally restricted to two and a half stories or 35 feet.

**Front Yard:**  
Minimum depth of 25 feet.

---

## R-3 Two-Family Dwelling and Apartment District

### Permitted Uses:
- All uses permitted in "R-1" district
- Two-family or duplex dwellings
- Apartment houses or multiple-family dwellings
- Boarding, lodging, and rooming houses
- Hospitals and clinics (with exceptions)
- Private clubs, fraternities, sororities, and lodges (excluding those whose chief activity is a customary business service)

---

## RV / Recreational Vehicle Regulations

### Article 12.05: Parking, Stopping and Standing

**Section 12.05.003 - Time Limits:**
"It is unlawful to park any vehicle on a street, alley, or other public place for more than 48 hours at any one time."

"For commercial motor vehicles over 24 feet in overall length, the time limit is restricted to two hours at any one time on any street or alley."

**Section 12.05.008 - Prohibited Parking:**
"Placing mounted or unmounted trailers on public street rights-of-way, public streets, and alleyways is prohibited."

"Placing campers or canopies within these public areas is also a violation."

### Residential Property Storage & Parking

**Note:** Multiple secondary sources reference Robstown regulations allowing:
- Temporary parking on residential lots for maximum **72 hours** for loading, cleaning, preparing for trip, winterizing, or preparing vehicle for off-premise storage
- Requirement for parking on "improved surface" (concrete, asphalt, brick, or comparable dust-free paving material)
- Prohibition on parking RVs on public thoroughfares in residential districts

## B-3 Central Business District

**Specific Prohibition:**  
Tourist courts and trailer courts are prohibited in the "B-3" Central Business District.

---

## Code Enforcement

The City of Robstown's Code Enforcement Division is responsible for upholding these zoning, housing, and property maintenance regulations.

Property owners and occupants are notified of violations and given a period, typically 10 days, to correct the issue before further enforcement actions, such as citations or abatement, are taken.
