## Key Financial Metrics for Hotel Loans

Lenders want to see:

### Primary Metrics:
- **DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio):** 1.25+ preferred
  - Formula: NOI / Annual Debt Service
- **Occupancy Rate:** 65%+ is healthy
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate):** Market competitive
- **RevPAR:** Occupancy × ADR
- **Cap Rate:** NOI / Property Value
- **Cash-on-Cash Return:** Annual Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested

### Secondary Metrics:
- Operating Expense Ratio
- Labor Cost %
- Marketing Cost %
- Maintenance Reserve
- Owner's Equity Position
