Nothing's more frustrating than turning the key and... nothing. We diagnose and fix no-start problems fast.

Common Reasons Your Car Won't Start

Dead Battery

Most common cause. Dim lights, clicking sound, or nothing at all.

Bad Starter

Clicking sound but engine doesn't turn over.

Alternator Failure

Battery keeps dying. Charging system not working.

Fuel Pump

Engine cranks but won't fire. No fuel reaching engine.

Ignition Switch

Nothing happens when you turn the key.

Security System

Anti-theft preventing start. Flashing security light.

Bad Sensor

Crankshaft position sensor or other critical sensors.
