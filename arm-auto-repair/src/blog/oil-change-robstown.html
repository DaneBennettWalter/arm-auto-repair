Why Regular Oil Changes Matter

Engine oil lubricates moving parts, reduces friction, cleans internal components, and helps manage engine temperature. Over time, oil breaks down and gets contaminated with dirt, metal particles, and combustion byproducts. Old oil can't protect your engine properly.

Skip oil changes and you risk:

  • Engine wear and damage from increased friction
  • Overheating from poor heat dissipation
  • Sludge buildup that clogs oil passages
  • Complete engine failure requiring expensive replacement
  • Voided warranty on newer vehicles

Types of Oil We Offer

Conventional Oil

Standard petroleum-based oil. Good for older vehicles and normal driving conditions. Typically changed every 3,000-5,000 miles.

Synthetic Blend Oil

Mix of conventional and synthetic oils. Better protection than conventional at a moderate price. Good for trucks and heavier vehicles. Change every 5,000-7,500 miles.

Full Synthetic Oil

Premium protection for high-performance and newer engines. Best for extreme temperatures (hot Texas summers!), towing, and extended oil change intervals. Change every 7,500-10,000 miles or per manufacturer recommendations.

Diesel Engine Oil

Specially formulated for diesel engines with higher detergent levels and additives. We're ASE certified for diesel service and stock quality diesel oils for trucks and heavy equipment.

How Often Should You Change Your Oil?

It depends on your vehicle, oil type, and driving conditions:

  • Conventional oil: Every 3,000-5,000 miles
  • Synthetic blend: Every 5,000-7,500 miles
  • Full synthetic: Every 7,500-10,000 miles
  • Always follow manufacturer recommendations in your owner's manual
