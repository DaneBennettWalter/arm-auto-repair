Transmission Repair in the Coastal Bend: What You Need to Know

Published April 10, 2025 | Transmission Service, Auto Repair

Your transmission is one of the most complex and expensive components in your vehicle. When transmission problems appear, fast professional diagnosis and repair can save you thousands of dollars.

Signs Your Transmission Needs Repair

  • Slipping gears - RPMs rise but vehicle doesn't accelerate
  • Delayed engagement - pause when shifting from park to drive
  • Rough shifting - jerking or clunking between gears
  • Leaking fluid - red or brown fluid under your vehicle
  • Burning smell - overheated transmission fluid
  • Check engine light - transmission codes stored
  • Grinding or shaking - especially in manual transmissions
  • Won't go into gear - stuck in one gear or neutral

Transmission Fluid Service

Regular transmission fluid service is the best preventive maintenance for your transmission. Clean fluid with proper additives keeps your transmission shifting smoothly and running cool.

When to Change Transmission Fluid:

  • Automatic transmissions: Every 30,000-60,000 miles (check your owner's manual)
  • Manual transmissions: Every 30,000-60,000 miles
  • Severe conditions: Towing, extreme heat, stop-and-go traffic requires more frequent service

South Texas heat is hard on transmissions. Regular fluid changes extend transmission life significantly.

  • Fluid service: Most affordable maintenance
  • Minor repairs: Sensors, solenoids, seals - moderate cost
  • Major repairs/rebuild: Significant cost but less than replacement
  • Replacement: Most expensive option

Prevent Transmission Problems

Simple habits that extend transmission life:

  • Let your vehicle warm up briefly before driving in cold weather
  • Don't shift into drive or reverse while still rolling
  • Service transmission fluid on schedule
  • Fix small leaks before they become big problems
  • Use your parking brake (takes load off transmission)
