Signs You Need Wheel Alignment in Corpus Christi

Published June 12, 2025 | Alignment, Tire Service

Does your vehicle pull to one side? Is your steering wheel crooked when driving straight? These are classic signs you need wheel alignment.

What Is Wheel Alignment?

Wheel alignment (also called tire alignment) adjusts the angles of your wheels to manufacturer specifications. Proper alignment ensures your tires make optimal contact with the road.

Signs You Need Alignment

  • Vehicle pulls left or right when driving straight
  • Steering wheel is off-center when driving straight
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear
  • Steering feels loose or vehicle wanders
  • Steering wheel vibrates
  • After hitting a pothole or curb

Why Alignment Matters

  • Tire life - misalignment destroys tires fast
  • Safety - proper handling and control
  • Fuel economy - misaligned wheels create drag
  • Comfortable driving - no pulling or fighting the wheel

What Causes Misalignment?

  • Hitting potholes or curbs
  • Normal wear over time
  • Worn suspension components
  • Accidents or impacts

How Often Should You Get Alignment?

Generally every 2-3 years, or:

  • When you notice pulling or uneven wear
  • After suspension or steering repairs
  • After hitting something hard
  • When getting new tires
