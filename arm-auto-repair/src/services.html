Our Services

Complete auto repair for gas and diesel vehicles in Robstown and the Coastal Bend

Engine Services

Engine Diagnostics

Advanced computer diagnostics to identify engine issues quickly and accurately. We use professional-grade scan tools to read trouble codes, monitor live data, and pinpoint problems in your gas or diesel engine.

Signs you need this: Check engine light, unusual noises, loss of power, rough idle

Engine Repair (Gas)

Expert gasoline engine repair from minor issues to major overhauls. Our ASE certified technicians handle everything from valve adjustments to complete engine repairs.

Signs you need this: Oil leaks, overheating, misfiring, poor fuel economy

Engine Repair (Diesel)

Specialized diesel engine service for trucks and heavy-duty vehicles. We're ASE certified for diesel repair and understand the unique needs of diesel engines.

Signs you need this: Hard starting, black smoke, fuel system issues, turbo problems

Engine Rebuilds

Complete engine rebuild services to restore your vehicle's power and reliability. More cost-effective than replacement, expertly executed.

Signs you need this: High mileage, major internal wear, excessive oil consumption

Timing Belt/Chain Replacement

Critical timing system service to prevent catastrophic engine damage. We replace timing belts and chains according to manufacturer specifications.

Signs you need this: Mileage interval reached, ticking noise, visible belt wear

Head Gasket Repair

Professional head gasket replacement and cylinder head resurfacing to fix coolant and oil leaks between engine block and cylinder head.

Signs you need this: White exhaust smoke, overheating, coolant in oil, loss of compression

Brake Services

Brake Inspection

Comprehensive brake system inspection including pads, rotors, calipers, brake lines, and fluid. We check for wear and safety issues.

When you need this: Annual safety check, before long trips, or when brakes feel different

Brake Pad Replacement

Quality brake pad installation using premium parts. We replace pads before they damage your rotors, saving you money.

Signs you need this: Squealing, grinding, longer stopping distance, brake warning light

Rotor Resurfacing/Replacement

Rotor machining to restore smooth braking surfaces, or replacement when rotors are beyond service limits.

Signs you need this: Vibration when braking, pulsating brake pedal, grooved rotors

Brake Fluid Flush

Complete brake fluid replacement to maintain proper brake system function and prevent corrosion.

When you need this: Every 2-3 years, dark/contaminated fluid, spongy brake pedal

ABS Diagnostics & Repair

Anti-lock brake system diagnostics and repair. We service ABS sensors, modules, and hydraulic components.

Signs you need this: ABS warning light, pulsing brake pedal, loss of ABS function

Emergency Brake Repair

Parking brake adjustment, cable replacement, and repair to keep your vehicle secure when parked.

Signs you need this: Brake doesn't hold, stuck parking brake, warning light stays on

Maintenance Services

Oil Change (Conventional & Synthetic)

Regular oil changes with quality conventional or synthetic oil. We use the right oil for your vehicle and check all fluid levels.

When you need this: Every 3,000-7,500 miles depending on oil type and driving conditions

Filter Replacement

Air filter, fuel filter, and cabin air filter replacement to keep your engine breathing clean and your cabin comfortable.

Signs you need this: Reduced fuel economy, weak airflow from vents, dusty cabin

Fluid Services

Complete fluid maintenance including transmission fluid, coolant, power steering, and differential fluid service.

When you need this: Follow manufacturer intervals or when fluids are dark/low

Tune-Ups

Complete tune-up service including spark plugs, ignition system check, and engine performance optimization.

Signs you need this: Rough idle, hesitation, poor fuel economy, hard starting

Belt Replacement

Serpentine belt and accessory belt inspection and replacement to prevent breakdowns.

Signs you need this: Squealing noise, visible cracks, age/mileage interval reached

Battery Service

Battery testing, cleaning, and replacement. We check charging system health and prevent no-start situations.

Signs you need this: Slow cranking, dim lights, battery age over 3 years

Electrical Services

Electrical Diagnostics

Advanced electrical system diagnostics using professional tools to trace and repair electrical issues.

Signs you need this: Electrical accessories not working, intermittent issues, parasitic battery drain

Alternator Replacement

Alternator testing and replacement to keep your battery charged and electrical system powered.

Signs you need this: Battery warning light, dimming lights, dead battery, whining noise

Starter Replacement

Starter motor and solenoid service to ensure reliable starting every time.

Signs you need this: Clicking when starting, no crank, intermittent starting issues

Wiring Repair

Electrical wiring diagnostics and repair for damaged, corroded, or faulty wiring harnesses.

Signs you need this: Blown fuses, melted wires, electrical shorts, corrosion

Lighting Repair

Headlight, taillight, turn signal, and interior lighting diagnosis and repair.

Signs you need this: Lights not working, dim lights, flickering, bulb replacement needed

Computer Diagnostics

Electronic control module diagnostics and programming for modern vehicle computer systems.

Signs you need this: Multiple warning lights, drivability issues, communication errors

Transmission Services

Transmission Diagnostics

Comprehensive transmission testing to identify shifting issues, slipping, and internal problems.

Signs you need this: Slipping gears, delayed shifting, transmission warning light, unusual noises

Transmission Repair

Expert transmission repair for automatic and manual transmissions, from minor fixes to major repairs.

Signs you need this: Hard shifting, grinding, won't go into gear, fluid leaks

Transmission Rebuild

Complete transmission rebuild service to restore your transmission to like-new condition.

Signs you need this: Major transmission failure, slipping, no drive, internal damage

Clutch Replacement

Manual transmission clutch, pressure plate, and flywheel service for smooth shifting and engagement.

Signs you need this: Slipping clutch, hard pedal, grinding when shifting, burning smell

Differential Service

Differential fluid service and repair for rear-wheel and four-wheel drive vehicles.

Signs you need this: Whining noise, clunking, vibration, fluid leaks

Suspension & Steering Services

Wheel Alignment

Precision wheel alignment to prevent tire wear and ensure straight tracking and proper handling.

Signs you need this: Vehicle pulls to one side, uneven tire wear, steering wheel off-center

Shocks & Struts

Shock absorber and strut replacement to restore ride quality and handling.

Signs you need this: Bouncy ride, nose dives when braking, uneven tire wear, fluid leaks

Ball Joints

Ball joint inspection and replacement to maintain safe steering and suspension operation.

Signs you need this: Clunking noise, wandering steering, uneven tire wear

Tie Rods

Tie rod and tie rod end replacement for precise steering control.

Signs you need this: Loose steering, tire wear, alignment won't hold

Power Steering Repair

Power steering pump, rack, and hose service for smooth, easy steering.

Signs you need this: Hard steering, whining noise, fluid leaks, groaning when turning

Heating & Cooling Services

A/C Diagnostics & Repair

Complete air conditioning system diagnostics and repair to keep you cool in South Texas heat.

Signs you need this: No cold air, weak airflow, strange smells, A/C cycles on and off

A/C Recharge

Refrigerant recharge service to restore cooling performance when your A/C is low on refrigerant.

Signs you need this: A/C blows warm, reduced cooling, system takes long to cool

Radiator Repair

Radiator replacement and cooling system repair to prevent overheating.

Signs you need this: Coolant leaks, overheating, low coolant, visible damage

Cooling System Flush

Complete coolant flush and fill to maintain proper engine temperature and prevent corrosion.

When you need this: Every 30,000-50,000 miles or when coolant is dirty/rusty

Heater Repair

Heater core and climate control repair for those cold Texas mornings.

Signs you need this: No heat, coolant smell, foggy windows, coolant on passenger floor
