Section 7. Description
____ Does the descriptive narrative accurately reflect the
architectural classification checked?
____ Are the important features of the property identified in the
Description Section, including site, date, materials, style, size,
roof-shape, story, plan, windows, foundation, details, interior?
____ Does the Description include information about the setting,
environment, and or surrounding buildings/areas?

Section 8. Statement of Significance
____ Does the narrative clearly represent and convey the period
of significance checked? Has the period(s) been justified in
the Statement of Significance?
____ Does the specific date or date range reflect the property’s
period of historic significance?
____ Do the Areas of Significance reflect the significance of the
property, not just its function?
____ Is the Statement of Significance written in a clear and
complete manner?
____ Have all the applicable criteria been identified and
documented within the Statement of Significance?

____ Is the Description clear and complete? If the property has
been altered, is the difference between the original (or
historic) and the current condition and appearance clear?

____ Are any criteria exceptions (if applicable) justified according
to their specific requirements?

____ Does the description convey the significant qualities of the
property? Through what features? Do these features retain
integrity?

____ Does the context in which the property has been evaluated
as significant justify the local, state, or national level of
significance chosen for the property?

____ Have contributing and noncontributing features been
identified?

____ Does the integrity relate to the overall property, not its
features and parts?
