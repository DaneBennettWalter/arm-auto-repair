## Tiny Home Ordinances - Full Analysis

Dane requested full analysis of tiny home ordinances with direct citations.

**Finding:** Robstown has no specific tiny home ordinance. Tiny homes are regulated under existing mobile home, trailer, ADU, and residential construction ordinances depending on construction type.

**Four regulatory categories:**
1. **Tiny homes on wheels (THOWs):** Classified as "trailers" → PROHIBITED on individual lots
2. **HUD-code manufactured tiny homes:** ALLOWED (state law preemption)
3. **Site-built tiny homes:** ALLOWED as small single-family dwellings
4. **Tiny homes as ADUs:** UNCLEAR / LIKELY PROHIBITED for rental use

**Dane's observation:** "It is pretty restrictive. It is a low income town trying to control unregulated development."

### Key Findings:

#### 45% Tax Credit Confirmed:
- **Federal Historic Preservation Tax Credit:** 20% (income-producing buildings)
- **Texas Historic Preservation Tax Credit:** 25% (transferable/sellable)
- **Combined:** 45% of Qualified Rehabilitation Expenditures
- **Texas credit advantage:** Can be sold for cash (85-95% of face value)

**Example:** $500K renovation = $100K federal + $112.5K Texas (sold) = $212.5K benefit (42.5%)

#### Critical Gateway: National Register of Historic Places
**Both tax credits require:**
- Listed in National Register OR eligible for listing
- Secretary of Interior's Standards compliance
- Income-producing property (Hotel Brendle qualifies ✅)

**National Register Process:**
- Cost: $5,000-$15,000 (consultant preparation)
- Requirements: 50+ years old, historical/architectural significance
- Administrator: Texas Historical Commission → National Park Service
- Sequential: Research → Nomination → THC Review → State Board → NPS Approval

#### Economic Development Programs Identified:

**1. Community Development Block Grants (CDBG)**
- Texas TDHCA administers for non-entitlement communities
- Robstown qualifies (pop ~5,000)
- Requires City of Robstown as applicant (partnership)
- Economic development, infrastructure, job creation funding
- Amounts: $100K-$2M+ depending on program

**2. USDA Rural Development**
- Business & Industry Loan Guarantees (up to 80%)
- Rural Business Development Grants
- Robstown qualifies (under 50K population)
- Contact: Temple, TX office (254) 742-9700

**3. Opportunity Zones** (requires verification)
- Need to confirm if Robstown has designated OZ census tracts
- If yes: capital gains tax incentives for investors

**4. Texas Enterprise Zone Program**
- Sales tax refunds on building materials and equipment
- Requires designated zone + job creation
- Need to verify Robstown/Nueces County designation

**5. Texas Preservation Trust Fund Grants**
- THC administered
- $5K-$25K for preservation projects
- Requires National Register listing

**6. New Markets Tax Credit (NMTC)**
- 39% tax credit over 7 years
- Low-income community investments
- Complex, requires CDE intermediary
- Typically $2M+ projects

### Key Findings:

**Strict Formatting Requirements:**
- Every blank must be filled (enter "N/A" if not applicable)
- Must use exact NPS categories (Function, Architecture, Areas of Significance)
- Resource counts in Section 5 must match Section 7 narrative
- Summary paragraphs mandatory in Sections 7 & 8
- All alterations must be described and dated

**Photo Requirements (2024 Policy):**
- **Minimum:** 1600x1200 pixels
- **Preferred:** 3000x2000 pixels
- **DPI:** 300 ppi or larger
- Photo log mandatory (keyed to sketch map)
- Clear, descriptive, multiple angles

**Map Requirements:**
- USGS locational map (7.5 or 15 minute series)
- Sketch map with north arrow and scale
- Coordinates (lat/long or UTM)
- Legal boundary description

**Research Requirements:**
- Construction date, architect, builder
- Historical context and significance
- All alterations documented with dates
- Architectural style identification
- Period of significance justified
- Bibliography of sources used

### 11-Section Form Breakdown:

**Section 1: Name** - Historic name, site number
**Section 2: Location** - Address, county, coordinates
**Section 3: State Certification** - THC staff completes
**Section 4: NPS Certification** - NPS Keeper completes
**Section 5: Classification** - Ownership, category, resource count
**Section 6: Function/Use** - Historic and current functions (exact categories)
**Section 7: Description** - Architecture, materials, narrative with integrity statement
**Section 8: Significance** - Criteria, areas, period, narrative justification
**Section 9: Bibliography** - All sources cited
**Section 10: Geography** - Acreage, coordinates, boundary description/justification
**Section 11: Preparer** - Who completed the form
